Project components

The process

How the system responds

A simple, easy-to-explain flow for a classroom or family presentation.

STEP 01

Power on

The controller starts the modules and the LCD prompts: “Scan RFID or enter PIN”.

STEP 02

Check credentials

A recognised card or correct PIN displays “Access Granted” and gives one short beep.

STEP 03

Reject invalid access

An unknown card displays “Access Denied”; a wrong PIN displays “Wrong PIN”. Both trigger error beeps.

STEP 04

Keep monitoring gas

The gas sensor is checked continuously, including while someone is entering a PIN.

STEP 05

Raise an urgent alert

When the reading passes the configured threshold, the urgent LCD message and warning sound take priority.

STEP 06

Return to ready

After the reading returns below a calibrated reset level, the system can return to its ready screen.

Real-world safety matters

This is an educational concept, not a certified alarm. If you suspect a real gas leak, leave the area immediately, avoid flames and do not operate electrical switches. Contact local emergency services or your gas supplier from a safe location. Never rely on a hobby sensor as your only protection.
